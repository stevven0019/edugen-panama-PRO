Theme 1: "How Much Is the Pineapple?" (Lesson #2 - Reading Focus)

Stage 2: Official "Market Price List" Text

Read the authentic informational price list text from the local Panama fresh market:

Gist Reading Check: What is the main purpose of this text?

Stage 3: Sentence Completion & Price Answering

Read the sentences and choose the missing words from the word bank based on the Price List:

pineapple dollar cents bananas apples
1. The costs two dollars each ($2.00).
2. Apples cost one per pound ($1.00).
3. The price of one banana is fifty ($0.50).
4. Four cost two dollars in total ($0.50 x 4 = $2.00).

Stage 4: "My Shopping List Reading" (Pair Task)

Practice reading with a partner! Student A reads the shopping list, and Student B reads the matching prices from the price board:

🛒 Customer Reading Card (Student A)

"Good morning! I need one pineapple, please."
"I also need two pounds of apples."
"And three bananas, please."
"Thank you! What is my total price?"

📋 Market Clerk Card (Student B)

"A pineapple is $2.00."
"Apples are $1.00 per pound ($2.00 for two pounds)."
"Bananas are $0.50 each ($1.50 for three)."
"The total is $5.50 ($2.00 + $2.00 + $1.50). Thank you!"

Stage 5: Reading Exit Ticket • "Today's Special"

Read the new market stand sign below and answer the questions:

TODAY'S SPECIAL STAND SIGN:

• Mangoes: $1.50 each   •   Oranges: $0.75 each   •   Fresh Pineapple: $2.00 each

Question 1: How much is one mango according to "Today's Special"?
Question 2: "The fresh pineapple is three dollars ($3.00)." → True or False?
