👨‍🏫 Teacher's Audio Script & Pronunciation Guide (Click to expand)

Audio 1 Script (Vendor Announcements):
"Good morning, shoppers! Welcome to the Central Market. Here are today's fresh specials!
Item 1: Sweet fresh pineapples are only one dollar each! Yes, one dollar for a big pineapple.
Item 2: Crunchy red apples are one dollar each.
Item 3: Delicious ripe bananas are two dollars for a whole bunch.
Item 4: Fresh local cassava (yuca) is three dollars for a bag.
Item 5: Big baking potatoes are four dollars for a five-pound sack!"


Audio 2 Script (Mrs. Gonzalez's List):
"Let me check my shopping list before I go to the stall. Today for our family soup, I need one pineapple for juice. I need five apples for the children's snack. I need three bananas. I need four potatoes, and I need two bags of cassava (yuca). That's everything on my list!"


Audio 3 Script (Dialogue):
Cashier: "Hello! Good morning. Can I help you?"
Customer: "Yes, please! Excuse me, how much is the pineapple?"
Cashier: "It is one dollar."
Customer: "Great! And how many apples do you have?"
Cashier: "We have fresh red apples. How many do you need?"
Customer: "I need three apples and one banana, please."
Cashier: "Okay, one pineapple, three apples, and one banana. That is four dollars in total."
Customer: "Here is the money. Thank you!"
Cashier: "You are welcome! Have a nice day!"
