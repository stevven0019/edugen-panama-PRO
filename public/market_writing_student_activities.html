✍️ Stage 1: Warm-up & Teacher Handwriting Modeling

Observe how clear English sentences begin with a Capital Letter, leave finger spaces between words, spell vocabulary correctly, and end with a Period (.).

1. Model for Expressing Needs & Quantities: Capital Letter Start Plural -s Final Period
"I need two apples."
2. Model for Expressing Costs & Prices in USD: Singular Verb (costs) Currency in USD
"The pineapple costs three dollars."

Golden Rule: Write quantity first, then the item!

Customer
Hello! I need three apples. How much are they?
Vendor
They are two dollars ($2.00).
Customer
And one pineapple. How much is it?
Vendor
It is three dollars ($3.00).

📝 Stage 4: Performance · "My Market Trip Report"
