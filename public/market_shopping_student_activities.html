Stage 2: Official Market Dialogue Presentation

Listen to the customer and the seller at the fruit market. Click the dialogue lines to practice active speaking!

Seller "Hello! Can I help you?"
Customer "Yes, please. Excuse me, how much is the pineapple?"
Seller "It is one dollar ($1.00)."
Customer (or Seller) "Okay. And how many apples do you need?"
Customer "I need three apples and one banana, please."
Seller "Okay, three apples and one banana. That's four dollars ($4.00)."
Customer "Here is the money."
Seller "Thank you!"
Comprehension Question: What does the customer buy in the dialogue?

Stage 4: "Shopping at the Market" Role-Play Mission

Pair up! One student is the Customer with a shopping list; the other is the Seller / Cashier with prices.

1 Cassava Say: "I need one cassava."
Total Budget: $10.00 Polite: "Here is the money."

Stage 5: Formative Checklist & Exit Ticket

Verify your oral speaking competencies according to the 4th Grade CEFR A1.2 curriculum!

Evaluation Criteria Yes! A Little Need Practice
