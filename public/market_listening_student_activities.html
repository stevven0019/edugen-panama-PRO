The Supermarket Day • Listening Lab

Theme 1: "How Much Is the Pineapple?" (Lesson #1 - Listening Focus)

Stage 1: Flashcards & Sound Modeling

Touch each fruit flashcard to hear the native pronunciation. Listen attentively to the names of the 5 market fruits:

1. Pineapple
2. Apples
3. Banana
4. Mango
5. Watermelon
CCQ #1: (Teacher shows apple) "Is this an apple or a banana?"
CCQ #2: "Do we ask 'how much' for color or for price?"

Stage 2: Official Dialogue "At the Market"

Listen to the dialogue between the seller and the buyer. Click "Play Full Dialogue" or touch individual lines:

Seller "Hello! Welcome to the market!"
Buyer "Hello! How much is the pineapple?"
Seller "It's three dollars ($3.00)."
Buyer "Okay. And the apples?"
Seller "They are one dollar each ($1.00)."
Buyer "Hmm. Can I have a banana, please?"
Seller "Yes, here you go."
Buyer "Thank you!"
Gist Check: What are the two people talking about?

Stage 3: Auditory Discrimination & Price Matching

Practice hearing specific dollar amounts and matching the fruits to the correct prices:

Stage 4: "Shopping List Drawing" Mission

Listen to the friend dictating the shopping list. Fill the slots on your market stall with the items and prices heard:

"At the market, please buy one pineapple for $3. Also two apples for $1 each..."
1. Pineapple Price: $3.00
2. Apples (x2) Price: $1.00 each
3. Mango Price: $2.00
4. Banana Price: $1.00

Stage 5: Formative Market Quiz

Test your listening comprehension with these quick questions from the lesson plan:

Question A: Did the buyer ask for an apple or a mango?
Question B: "The watermelon is five dollars." → Is this statement True or False?
Question C: How much is the banana in the dialogue?
